## Business Overview

- **Business Name:** Dharmartha

- **Target Country / Region:** India

1. Build topical authority in Bitcoin-related topics specifically for Indian audiences

4. Establish trust as India's go-to Bitcoin knowledge source

## Tone & Style

- **Tone:** Educational, trustworthy, simplified, calm — NOT hype-driven
- **Reading level:** Accessible to a first-time crypto investor. Avoid heavy jargon. When jargon is used, explain it immediately.
- **CTA style:** Soft lead generation — "Want personalized Bitcoin guidance for the Indian market? Reach out to Dharmartha."
- **Content language:** English, India-localized — use INR examples, reference Indian regulations (RBI, SEBI, IT Act), mention popular Indian exchanges
- **Avoid:** Guaranteed returns, "get rich" language, speculation presented as fact
