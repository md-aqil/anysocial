# Business Brief — Vaclav Fashion

---

## Business Overview

- **Business Name:** Vaclav Fashion
- **Website:** https://www.vaclavfashion.com/
- **Industry / Niche:** Fashion & Apparel — Women's ethnic wear, luxury streetwear, men's suits, sustainable clothing
- **Target Country / Region:** India
- **Target Audience:** Indian women aged 20–35, urban professionals, and Gen Z fashion lovers who follow trends, value style, and increasingly care about sustainable choices. They shop online, follow fashion influencers, and look for both occasion wear and everyday stylish outfits.

---

## Business Goals (in priority order)

1. Drive direct sales through high-intent organic search traffic
2. Build Vaclav Fashion's authority as a premium Indian fashion brand
3. Rank on page 1 of Google India for fashion-related keywords
4. Grow organic traffic consistently through pillar content clusters
5. Attract Gen Z and millennial shoppers searching for style inspiration

## Tone & Style

- **Mixed tone:** Warm and trendy for Gen Z content; aspirational and elevated for luxury streetwear and men's suits; clean and conscious for sustainable fashion
- **Reading level:** Casual, conversational, easy-to-scan — use short paragraphs, bullet lists, and styled subheadings
- **CTA style:** Product-focused but soft — e.g., "Shop the look at Vaclav Fashion", "Explore our new collection"
- **Content language:** English, India-localized — reference Indian festivals (Diwali, Navratri, weddings), Indian cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru), Indian seasons (monsoon, summer, festive)
- **Visual notes:** Recommend at least 4 images per article (outfit photos, fabric close-ups, styled looks). Alt text should include primary keyword naturally.

## Keywords to AVOID

- "cheap fashion" or "cheap clothes" — off-brand, undermines luxury positioning
- "fast fashion" as a positive — conflicts with sustainable messaging
- Specific competitor brand names as primary keywords
- Overly generic terms like "buy clothes online" without a strong modifier
